### Usage boundaries

- Use only one background line treatment on a chapter at a time. The PNG plate and responsive SVG versions are alternate implementations, not stacked effects.
- Keep curves outside text, venue addresses, RSVP controls, and image subjects. Do not animate in Phase 2.5.
- The divider and corner frame are explicitly edge-dependent; do not use either as free-floating background art.
- The booklet, silver bands, and ribbon/pen are optional standalone cutouts; show at most one in a section and keep them smaller than user photos.
- No logo, text, people, botanical motifs, or couple-identifying imagery are present. The silver-band cutout is the only explicit ring motif.
- The SVGs are small, authored vector artwork; the PNG is the only generated raster. No external references, fonts, or source images were used.
